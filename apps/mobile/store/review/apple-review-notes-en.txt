Tang Ren Daily is a news and community app. All news sections can be reviewed without signing in. From the My tab, choose Continue as guest or return to the news tabs.

To review authenticated features, use the non-expiring review account entered in App Review Information > Sign-in information. Open My > Sign in, enter that identifier and password, and tap Sign in / Register. This account is pre-created. No OTP, MFA, subscription, location restriction, invitation, or external hardware is required.

Authenticated review paths:
1. Community: open Community to create a post; open a post to comment, reply, like, report, or unpublish your own post.
2. News comments: open any news article and scroll to Comments to comment, reply, like, or report.
3. Cloud library: save an article, then open My > Favorites or My > Reading history.
4. Push: open My > Push notifications and explicitly enable this device. The app does not request notification permission on first launch.
5. Account deletion: open My > Account and privacy > Delete account. The final action requires typing DELETE and permanently removes the signed-in account.
