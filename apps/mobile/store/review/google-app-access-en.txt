Tang Ren Daily is partly restricted by sign-in. News browsing in Home, U.S., Immigration and Legal Updates is available without signing in.

For all restricted features, use the non-expiring review account stored in the Google Play App access credential fields. Open My > Sign in, enter the supplied email or phone identifier and password, and tap Sign in / Register. The account is already provisioned. No OTP, MFA, subscription, location restriction, invitation, external hardware, QR code, or separate organization account is required.

After sign-in, reviewers can access every restricted area:
1. Community posting and interactions: Community > Create post, or open a post to comment, reply, like, report, or unpublish your own post.
2. News comments: open any article and scroll to Comments.
3. Synced content: save an article, then open My > Favorites or My > Reading history.
4. Push settings: My > Push notifications. Permission is requested only after the reviewer explicitly enables the device.
5. Account deletion: My > Account and privacy > Delete account. Type DELETE only if permanent deletion is intended.

These instructions remain valid regardless of location. If Google Play presents separate username and password fields, enter the credentials there; do not place them in the public release notes.
